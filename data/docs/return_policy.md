# Return Policy

All electronics can be returned within 14 days of purchase. They must be in their original packaging with all accessories included. A 15% restocking fee applies to all opened electronics, unless the item is proven defective.
Apparel can be returned within 30 days of purchase, provided the tags are still attached and the item is unworn.
All clearance items are final sale and cannot be returned.

## Exceptions
- Apple products (e.g. MacBook Air M3) must be returned within 7 days.
- Vacuum cleaners (e.g. Dyson) cannot be returned if they have been used, due to hygiene reasons.
